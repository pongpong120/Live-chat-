Privacy & Safety Center

🛡️ Account & Security

Authentication uses your signed session. OTP/password credentials are not shown to other users. Use Logout on shared devices and Delete Account only when you are certain.

🚫 Blocked Users

Blocking prevents private text/photo messaging. The blocked relationship is enforced by the server, not only by the screen. You can unblock the user from their profile or private chat.

💬 Private Messaging

Private messages are limited to the conversation participants. Clear Messages removes the saved conversation timeline for that chat. Do not send passwords, OTPs or payment credentials.

📞 Private Audio Calls

Private audio calling is available only when both users follow each other. Calls work from the private chat even after both users leave a room.

🔔 Notifications

Push notification preference is controlled from Settings. Device-level notification permission is controlled by Android/browser settings.

🧹 Cache & Local Data

Clean Cache removes supported local browser cache entries while preserving the active login/session keys used by this app.

💎 Payments & Virtual Items

Diamonds, gifts and other virtual items are governed by the applicable purchase/store rules. Keep receipts for payment disputes and report duplicate or unauthorized charges to support promptly.

📝 Feedback

Feedback submitted from the app is shown to authorized Control Panel staff and is automatically removed after 7 days. Never include secrets or payment credentials.
