PingPong Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: 15 October 2026

This policy describes the categories of information PingPong may process to provide voice rooms, profiles, messaging, virtual items, account security, and support. Before release, the operator must verify this page against the exact deployed build, every enabled third-party SDK, and the Google Play Data safety form.

Information processed

  • Account and profile: mobile number or account identifier, display name, biography, profile image, and profile settings.
  • Authentication and security: OTP/login and session data, device/network diagnostics, abuse-prevention signals, and security logs.
  • App activity and user content: room participation, voice-room metadata, messages, reports, blocks, and moderation actions needed to run and protect the service.
  • Virtual items and transactions: Diamond/Bean balances, gifts, recharge/order status, and transaction records needed to deliver purchases, prevent fraud, and resolve disputes.
  • Support requests: details you send to customer support.

Microphone access is used when you choose a voice-room feature. Do not grant the permission unless you intend to use that feature. The app should request permissions only when needed.

Why information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts, provide voice rooms and messaging, maintain balances and transactions, prevent fraud and abuse, enforce community rules, troubleshoot reliability, and answer support requests.

Service providers and sharing

Some information may be processed by infrastructure or service providers that support authentication, hosting, voice communication, object storage, or payment processing when those features are enabled. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect users, the service, or legal rights. The operator must keep this description aligned with the providers and SDKs enabled in the deployed version; no sale of personal information is intended by this policy.

Security and retention

Production traffic must use HTTPS/TLS and secrets must be stored server-side, not in the APK or public source archive. Information is retained only for as long as needed to operate the service, meet legitimate legal/accounting obligations, resolve disputes, enforce security, and prevent fraud. Actual retention periods and exceptions must be documented by the operator before release.

Account deletion and choices

When signed in, use the account deletion option in Settings. If you cannot sign in, see the account deletion request page or contact support. Valid requests are processed after ownership verification. Some transaction, legal, fraud-prevention, or security records may need to be retained for legitimate reasons; retained data should be limited to that purpose.

Children and safety

PingPong must apply the age, child-safety, reporting, blocking, and moderation requirements relevant to its actual audience and features. Do not publish until these controls and the Play Console declarations have been verified.

Contact

Privacy and account requests: pingpongappsupport@gmail.com
