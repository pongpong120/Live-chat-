PingPong account and data deletion

This page explains how to request deletion of your PingPong account and associated personal data. You do not need to reinstall the app to make a request.

Option 1: Delete from the app

  1. Sign in to the PingPong account you want to delete.
  2. Open your Profile or Settings screen.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm the request.

The authenticated app request uses DELETE /api/account.

Option 2: Request deletion if you cannot sign in

Email pingpongappsupport@gmail.com with the subject “PingPong account and data deletion request”. Provide your account mobile number in international format and username if known. Do not send your password, OTP, or payment credentials. Support may request reasonable verification before acting.

Data deleted and data that may be retained

After ownership is verified and the request is processed, the account profile and personal data associated with the account are deleted or de-identified where applicable. Certain transaction, accounting, fraud-prevention, dispute, security, or legally required records may be retained for the period required for those purposes. Any retained data should be restricted to that purpose and deleted when no longer required.

Timing and status

Support will confirm receipt and may contact you for verification. Processing time can depend on verification and legally required retention. Contact the email above to ask for request status.

Privacy policy

Read the PingPong Privacy Policy for information about data processing, retention, and contact details.
